ISA Bridge Test

Retiring before private pensions unlock requires enough accessible money for every intervening year. The model stops contributions at the tested retirement age, spends the target from ISA, and then checks the remaining assets at 57.

This is an intentionally simple deterministic bridge test, not a full decumulation simulation. It uses your selected real return and assumes spending stays constant in real terms.

Access Windows

Age 57+ — SIPP and workplace pension accessible (25% tax-free, rest as income)
Any age — ISA accessible tax-free (capital gains shielded)
Age 68 — State Pension begins (£12,547.60/yr full 2026/27 rate; actual entitlement varies)

Educational tool only. Not financial advice. Assumptions: real returns above inflation; contributions stay constant in real terms until the tested retirement age; full new State Pension £241.30/week (£12,547.60/year) from age 68; SIPP/workplace pension accessible from 57. Tax uses 2026/27 England, Wales and Northern Ireland bands, includes the Personal Allowance taper and treats State Pension as taxable. The FI gate uses sustainable pension income after tax-free cash is exhausted; it does not repeat 25% tax-free withdrawals forever. Scottish rates, scheme-specific protected pension ages, fees and prior crystallisations are not modelled. Figures are real unless stated. Check your own State Pension forecast and scheme rules.
